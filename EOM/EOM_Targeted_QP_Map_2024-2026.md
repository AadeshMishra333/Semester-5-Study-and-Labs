1. Fayol + Mintzberg.
2. Functional and matrix departmentation diagrams.
3. SWOT/TOWS + BCG matrices.
4. Types of plans + MBO + quantitative/qualitative objectives.
5. CSR + ethics.

8. Porter + Blue Ocean + systems approach + strategic planning.
9. Finish MCQ-only basics: managerial skills, business/industry classification, external environment, Urwick principles and management functions.

## 3. Topic-wise descriptive question bank

### A. Henri Fayol's 14 Principles - asked in every paper (21 marks)

Use this answer format every time: **quoted case line -> principle name -> one-line meaning -> application to the case**.

- **P1 Q12 - 3 marks:** From the Infosys case, quote lines and identify/explain three Fayol principles. Case cues: specialized roles based on expertise; one unified strategic plan across global operations; competitive compensation reflecting contribution.
- **P2 Q12 - 3 marks:** From the Infosys case, quote lines and identify/explain three principles. Case cues: adherence to policies and mutual respect; employees encouraged to take initiative; clearly defined chain of superiors.
- **P3 Q12 - 3 marks:** From the Nexa Manufacturing case, quote lines and identify/explain three principles used by the new CEO. Cues: specialized production teams; a single strategic plan for EV components; strategic decisions centralized while operational autonomy is delegated.
- **P4 Q13 - 3 marks:** From Mark Schneider's Nestle case, quote lines and identify/explain three principles. Cues: clear organizational structure and reporting line; accountability for results; employees aligned with specified roles.
- **P5 Q13 - 3 marks:** From the OYO case, quote lines and identify/explain three principles. Cues: clear hierarchy/chain; each person has one reporting line; long-term roles provide workforce stability.
- **P6 Q13 - 3 marks:** From the PhysicsWallah case, quote lines and identify/explain three principles. Cues: personal interests aligned with organizational mission; teamwork and collective morale; strategic centralization with operational empowerment.
- **P7 Q13 - 3 marks:** From the SkyLine Electronics case, identify any four principles and justify them with case lines. Cues: measurable target; authority granted to project lead; one engineer-one senior lead; designated place for tools/documents; organizational interest before ego; fair treatment and team enthusiasm.

### B. Mintzberg's Managerial Roles - asked in every paper (21 marks)

Always state both the **main category** (interpersonal/informational/decisional) and the **specific sub-role**.

- **P1 Q13 - 3 marks:** In the Quantum Solutions case, identify three main roles and sub-roles with quoted evidence. Cues: daily briefings; Guest of Honor at events; balancing investment among projects.
- **P2 Q13 - 3 marks:** In the Quantum Solutions case, identify three roles. Cues: networking with stakeholders; communicating vision publicly; investing in a risky autonomous-driving opportunity.
- **P3 Q13 - 3 marks:** In the Satya Nadella/Microsoft case, identify three roles. Cues: official ceremonies and summits; public/investor/media statements; negotiation of partnerships, acquisitions and enterprise contracts.
- **P4 Q12 - 3 marks:** In the Zomato CEO case, identify three roles. Cues: scanning market/customer trends; communicating sustainability initiatives internally; resolving resistance over costs.
- **P5 Q12 - 3 marks:** In the Byju Raveendran case, identify three roles. Cues: inaugurating offices and meeting stakeholders; representing Byju's at global forums/media; bargaining with venture capital firms.
- **P6 Q12 - 3 marks:** In the Deepinder Goyal case, identify three roles. Cues: ceremonial representation; networking with partners/providers/investors; communicating strategies and initiatives to public/media.
- **P7 Q14 - 3 marks:** For four stages in the RoboDog Tech change case, identify the main category and sub-role with evidence: mediating QA-developer conflict; reallocating funds; defending the transition to investors; negotiating with a cloud provider.

### C. Departmentation and organization structures - asked in every paper (17 marks)

- **P1 Q19 - 2 marks:** Draw a structure for Mega Bazaar with functional departments and product subsections that enables product-profit responsibility. **Target: matrix departmentation.**
- **P2 Q19 - 2 marks:** Draw the departmentation structure described as allowing economies of scale within departments but causing decisions to pile up at the top. **Target: functional departmentation.**
- **P3 Q19 - 2 marks:** Identify and draw the structure in which the CEO has VPs for Operations, Finance and HR, with specialized teams below functional managers. **Target: functional departmentation.**
- **P4 Q16 - 3 marks:** An IT startup has functional experts assigned across three client projects. Identify and draw the structure; state two advantages. **Target: matrix departmentation.**
- **P5 Q16 - 3 marks:** Identify and sketch the structure that provides economies of scale, deep functional knowledge and achievement of functional goals. **Target: functional departmentation.**
- **P6 Q19 - 2 marks:** Tata Motors assigns employees from Design, Production, Finance and Marketing to EV, truck and SUV projects. Identify and draw the structure. **Target: matrix departmentation.**
- **P7 Q16 - 3 marks:** Design RB Automotive's structure using four functional departments across three projects so project profit/cost responsibility is visible; state one disadvantage. **Target: matrix departmentation.**

### D. SWOT and TOWS - four 4-mark questions (16 marks)

For each question: classify numbered facts into a labelled SWOT matrix, then formulate the requested TOWS strategies. Internal = strengths/weaknesses; external = opportunities/threats.

- **P2 Q11 - 4 marks:** MRF Tyres - classify 12 facts into SWOT and create two strategies to tackle weaknesses using TOWS.
- **P3 Q11 - 4 marks:** Nestle - classify 13 facts into SWOT and create two TOWS strategies; enter statement numbers in a labelled matrix.
- **P4 Q11 - 4 marks:** Tata Motors - classify 12 facts into SWOT and create two TOWS strategies using only statement numbers in the matrix.
- **P5 Q11 - 4 marks:** Hindustan Unilever - classify 12 facts into SWOT and create two strategies addressing weaknesses.

### E. BCG Matrix / Boston Box - five papers (14 marks)

Prepare quadrant identification and strategy together: **Star, Cash Cow, Question Mark, Dog** plus **build/invest, hold, harvest or divest**.

- **P1 Q11 - 4 marks:** Classify four Hassan detergent products from market growth and market traction/share, and develop appropriate BCG strategic options.
- **P2 Q15 - 3 marks:** Explain the use of the BCG Matrix with a suitable example.
- **P3 Q18 - 2 marks:** Explain how the product life cycle is integrated into the application of the Boston Box.
- **P5 Q18 - 2 marks:** Conduct a Boston Box analysis with strategies for Dabur Chyawanprash (high share, high growth) and Dabur Real Juice (low share, low growth).
- **P7 Q15 - 3 marks:** Draw the BCG Matrix; classify Cloud Storage, Legacy Antivirus, Internal Disc Drives and AI Personal Assistants from the supplied growth/share data; recommend a BCG strategy for each.

### F. Types of plans, objectives, MBO and strategic planning (23 marks)

#### Types of plans - 13 marks

- **P4 Q15 - 3 marks:** Give one top-level and one lower-level FMCG example for each of six types of plans.
- **P5 Q15 - 3 marks:** From BoAt practices, identify and explain three plans: strict conduct/anti-harassment compliance; long-term growth in digital transformation/AI; recruiting 35-40% through campus placements annually.
- **P6 Q15 - 3 marks:** From Blinkit practices, identify and explain three plans: stepwise order-processing protocol; mandatory safety/customer-service guidelines; loyalty and marketing programs.
- **P7 Q11 - 4 marks:** Identify and explain the plan represented by each Organic Manipal guideline: a three-year market-leadership target; priority for local ingredients; five-step quality-check sequence; no-smoking prohibition.

#### Management by Objectives - 5 marks

- **P3 Q15 - 3 marks:** Draw a neat block diagram showing how an MIT Head of Department uses the process of managing and appraising by objectives.
- **P7 Q18 - 2 marks:** Identify the system where engineers and managers jointly set quarterly milestones and track progress; state one advantage and one disadvantage. **Target: MBO.**

#### Objectives and strategic planning - 5 marks

- **P1 Q18 - 2 marks:** Explain quantitative objectives from an organizational perspective and give a company performance example.
- **P4 Q18 - 2 marks:** Illustrate the Strategic Planning Process with a neat block diagram.
- Strategic management process is also directly tested in **P7 Q6 (0.5 mark MCQ)**.

### G. Span of control, organizing process and formal/informal organization (18 marks)

#### Span of control - 10 marks

- **P1 Q16 - 3 marks:** Compare managers supervising five and fifteen employees; explain three factors influencing their span of control.
- **P2 Q16 - 3 marks:** Give three advantages and three disadvantages each of wide and narrow spans of control.
- **P4 Q19 - 2 marks:** Identify Sneha's span from an inexperienced, poorly trained team requiring constant guidance; justify and suggest a solution.
- **P7 Q17 - 2 marks:** Decide which of Data Entry (standardized/repetitive) and R&D (complex/non-routine) can use a wide span; justify and give three other determining factors.

#### Organizing process and formal/informal organization - 8 marks

- **P3 Q16 - 3 marks:** Explain the steps in the organizing process and give two differences between formal and informal organization.
- **P5 Q19 - 2 marks:** Explain the four-step organizing process with an example.
- **P6 Q16 - 3 marks:** Using a corporate example, explain the four-step organizing process and the interplay of formal and informal structures.

### H. Corporate Social Responsibility - five papers (13 marks)

- **P1 Q14 - 3 marks:** Explain the significance of social responsibility to a corporation; argue whether businesses should be socially responsible, with an example.
- **P2 Q14 - 3 marks:** Discuss three arguments each for and against CSR.
- **P3 Q14 - 3 marks:** Explain three key drivers of CSR and give one corporate example for each.
- **P6 Q17 - 2 marks:** Analyze how four key CSR drivers influence strategic and ethical decision-making, with corporate examples.
- **P7 Q19 - 2 marks:** Explain any two key CSR drivers and provide one corporate example for each.

### I. Ethics, environment, culture and international management (15 marks)

#### Ethics - 4 marks

- **P1 Q17 - 2 marks:** Describe two ways managers create an organizational environment that fosters ethical decision-making.
- **P2 Q17 - 2 marks:** Explain the two basic types of moral theories in normative ethics, with one example each. The MCQs additionally require utilitarianism, rights and justice theories.

#### Environment, culture and international styles - 11 marks

- **P4 Q14 - 3 marks:** State six differences between Korean and Japanese management styles.
- **P4 Q17 - 2 marks:** Explain how ethical considerations and social influences in the external environment affect organizational functioning.
- **P5 Q14 - 3 marks:** Explain three dimensions through which national culture affects employee behavior and management practices, using India as context.
- **P6 Q14 - 3 marks:** Analyze and compare three international business management styles across six management aspects.

### J. Theory Z - three papers (7 marks)

- **P2 Q18 - 2 marks:** Explain the management theory developed by William Ouchi in the 1980s.
- **P3 Q17 - 2 marks:** Explain Ouchi's concept that promotes stable employment, high productivity, morale and satisfaction.
- **P7 Q12 - 3 marks:** Explain Theory Z and discuss its relevance to organizational commitment in today's competitive environment.

### K. Porter, Blue Ocean and other strategic tools (9 marks)

#### Porter generic strategies - 4 marks

- **P6 Q11 - 4 marks:** From four corporate examples, identify and explain the Porter strategy used by FabIndia, Reliance Jio, Big Bazaar and Tanishq.

#### Blue Ocean Strategy - 5 marks

- **P1 Q15 - 3 marks:** Give four differences between Red Ocean and Blue Ocean strategies; explain creation of a blue ocean with an example.

### L. Systems approach - 2 marks plus recurring MCQs

## 5. Final exam-preparation checklist

### Learn as keyword sheets

- Fayol: 14 names + meaning + case signals.
- Mintzberg: 3 categories + 10 sub-roles + case signals.
- Urwick: 10 principles + distinguishing phrases.
- Types of plans and types of objectives.
- Managerial skills and levels of management.
- Business vs non-business; primary/secondary/tertiary/quaternary sectors.
- External-environment factors and national-culture dimensions.
- Utilitarian, rights and justice theories.

### Practise as case-identification templates

- Fayol and Mintzberg cases.
- SWOT/TOWS statement classification.
- BCG quadrant and strategy selection.
- Plan identification from organizational statements.
- Wide/narrow span justification.
- Porter strategy identification.

### Practise as diagrams

- SWOT/TOWS, BCG, functional departmentation, matrix departmentation, MBO process, organizing process, systems approach and strategic planning process.

### Best answer format by marks

- **2 marks:** definition/identification + two sharp points or a labelled mini-diagram.
- **3 marks:** short definition + three headed points, or three case mappings with quoted evidence.
- **4 marks:** complete labelled matrix/diagram + classification + strategy/recommendation as requested.
